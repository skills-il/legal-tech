# Domain checklist: pre-signing employment-contract review

Used to audit this skill's coverage. Each row names where the skill covers it. Rate tables are
listed by every category the rate varies by.

## Must cover (core)

| Topic | Varies by | Where covered |
|-------|-----------|---------------|
| Minimum wage floor | Age (adult, under 16, under 17, under 18), scope, monthly vs hourly (182 / 186 hours; 173 for youth) | SKILL.md red flags; `numerical-sanity-checks.md` |
| Mandatory pension | Insured at hire or not (day 1 vs 6 months), age threshold (21 men, 20 women), product type (pension fund vs other, 5% floor and disability cover), salary base and the average-wage ceiling, employee choice of product and institution | SKILL.md mandatory protections, Gotcha 7; `high-value-clause-traps.md` §1 |
| Section 14 | Collective agreement or General Authorization, the waiver's salary definition against the severance base, 6% vs 8.33%, unfunded waiting-period salary | SKILL.md; `section-14-trap-guide.md` |
| Advance notice | Monthly vs hourly worker, tenure band, contractual longer period | `hoda-at-mukdemet-table.md`; `additional-clause-checks.md` §3; script |
| Annual leave | Calendar-year seniority, statute gross ladder, Shortened Work Week order net ladder and its exclusions, minors (18 days), carry-over and redemption | `numerical-sanity-checks.md`; `additional-clause-checks.md` §4; script |
| Sick pay | Payment ladder (day 1, days 2-3, day 4+), accrual and 90-day cap | `numerical-sanity-checks.md`; script |
| Dmei havra'a | Seniority ladder (5 to 10 days), private vs public rate, 12-month qualifying period | SKILL.md; `numerical-sanity-checks.md` |
| Hours and overtime | 5-day vs 6-day week daily norm (8.6 / 7.6 vs 8 / 7), daily 125% / 150% split, the 42-hour extension order, global overtime, s.30(a)(5) | SKILL.md; `high-value-clause-traps.md` §8 |
| Weekly rest | 36 hours, permit requirement, 150% plus rest in lieu, hiring-condition undertakings | `additional-clause-checks.md` §2 |
| Deductions from pay | s.25 closed list, the quarter cap, s.25(b) final wage, deduction vs repayment undertaking | SKILL.md; `red-flag-checklist.md` A9 |
| Written terms | Notice to Employee Law list, 30 days (7 for minors), consequences | `notice-to-employee-disclosures.md` |
| Restrictive covenants | Non-compete (legitimate interests), non-solicitation, confidentiality | SKILL.md; `additional-clause-checks.md` §1 |
| IP | Service inventions (Patents Law s.132), works (Copyright Law s.34), compensation waiver (s.134) | SKILL.md; `standard-negotiation-language.md` §8 |
| Protected employees | Pregnancy and fertility treatment, miluim (s.41A and the 2026 extension order) | SKILL.md; `high-value-clause-traps.md` §6 |
| Privacy and monitoring | Email, location, biometric attendance, remote-work tracking | `additional-clause-checks.md` §9 |
| Who the employer is | Direct employer vs manpower contractor, employee vs contractor | SKILL.md; `additional-clause-checks.md` §5 |
| Travel reimbursement | Daily cap vs monthly pass | SKILL.md |

## Out of scope (explicit)

| Topic | Why, and where it goes |
|-------|------------------------|
| Drafting a contract for the employer | `israeli-employment-contracts` |
| Post-hire enforcement: unpaid wages or pension, hearing procedure, resignation deemed dismissal under Severance Pay Law ss.6, 7 and 8 (health, childbirth, relocation) | Events after hiring, not contract terms; `israeli-workplace-rights-navigator`. Reviewed 2026-10-25: a pre-signing user does not ask these, and the contract-side hook (s.11(a) against unilateral variation) is covered |
| Net pay, tax credits, National Insurance arithmetic | `israeli-payroll-calculator` |
| Unemployment benefits | `israeli-unemployment-benefits-navigator` |
| Tax planning of an equity exit | A tax adviser |
| Remote work from abroad and foreign-national employment (visa, tax and National Insurance limbs) | Reviewed 2026-10-25: a minority of candidates ask, and no primary source on whether cogent protections follow the employee abroad was verified, so nothing is asserted. Flag a "work from anywhere" clause and route the tax side to a tax skill |
