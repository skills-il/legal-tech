# Forms, codes and clocks (debtor side)

Every clock below runs from **service of the azhara** (hamtzaat ha-azhara), not from the
date printed on it and not from the day the debtor noticed the file.

## Forms

| Form | Purpose | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 218 | Objection to execution of a bill, or to a fixed-sum claim | Same form for both routes. 3 copies. Needs a witnessed affidavit |
| 236 | Paraati claim (debt already discharged) | Mark relief number 119. 2 copies |
| 214 | Cancel, stay or renew proceedings and restrictions | Online code 311. Per file, not per debtor |
| 233 | Payment order, or change of an existing one | Attach form 529 |
| 529 | Questionnaire and confidentiality waiver | Signed by the debtor, attached to form 233 |

## Clocks

| Days | What | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| 30 | Objection deadline | Bill files and fixed-sum files ONLY |
| 20 | Payment-order request | Money judgment and bill files |
| 30 | Payment-order request | Fixed-sum files |
| 21 | Ability examination, and the paraati exemption from it | All money files |
| 14 | Creditor delivers the court decision back to the office | After the court rules on an objection |
| none | Paraati claim | Any stage, any time |

## Challenge route by file type

| File type | Objection? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bill or check | Yes, form 218 | Grounds include failure of consideration, forgery, theft, no signature, defective endorsement, security check |
| Fixed-sum claim | Yes, form 218 | Adds technical grounds: over the 75,000 NIS ceiling, or not calculable by simple arithmetic |
| Money judgment | No | Paraati, set-aside of a default judgment, appeal, or stay of execution |
| Maintenance | Separate track | Wage protection does not apply. Arrest is available as a remedy |
| Performance of an act | Separate track | Out of scope for this skill |

## The four restrictions

Exit from the country; passport or travel document; debit and credit cards; founding or
holding office in a corporation.

There is no driving-licence restriction in enforcement proper. Cancellation is on form 214
and is granted where the debt is paid, a payment order is being complied with, or an
approved agreement with the creditor is being complied with.

## Two traps worth restating

1. A payment order, once granted, does **not** lift existing restrictions or attachments.
   Each needs its own request.
2. A restriction can be re-imposed immediately and with no warning if compliance lapses.
