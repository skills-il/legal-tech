# Forms, codes and clocks (debtor side)

Every clock below runs from **service of the azhara** (hamtzaat ha-azhara), not from the
date printed on it and not from the day the debtor noticed the file.

## Counting

- The day of service is not counted, so the deadline is the service date plus N days.
- A last day that is a Friday, Saturday, holiday or recess day may move to the next working day
  (Interpretation Law s.10; Enforcement (Recess) Regulations reg. 3 treats a last-day Friday as a
  recess day). For the objection only, the office recess may not be counted (Enforcement
  Regulations reg. 128(ב)). Give the user the raw date and do not plan on either.
- Since 31.8.2026 an online filing after 23:59 on Sunday to Thursday, or on a Friday, Saturday or
  rest day, counts as filed on the next weekday (reg. 15א2).
- Filing any request in the file counts as full service of the azhara on that day (s.7).

## Reservist relief (temporary order, in force until 28.2.2027)

Protected: a debtor who served in the reserves (or volunteered) during the temporary order, and
that person's spouse. The debtor notifies the office and attaches the call-up order. Protection
runs for three months from the start of the reserve service or of the temporary order, whichever
is later, and is extended by a further three months if service has not ended and the debtor files
a notice with the call-up order. During it, the steps in reg. 3(א1)(2) of the special-emergency
regulations are not taken: operational steps (seizure of movables or a vehicle, eviction, bring
and arrest orders), third-party attachments, restriction requests and requests for information
about the debtor. A step taken during the period before the notice can be cancelled on request.
The registrar may, at the creditor's request, decide otherwise for special reasons. Maintenance
debt is excluded, except maintenance collected by Bituach Leumi. The relief covers collection
steps; nothing in it extends the debtor's own deadlines.

## Payment-order ceiling (s.7א1, s.7א2)

The payment period may not exceed three years for debt up to 100,000 NIS, or four years above that. The registrar may extend it by further periods totalling up to three more years when satisfied the debtor can pay within the longer period, and longer periods are possible only if creditor and debtor both agree. A debtor who cannot pay even within those limits files a notice saying so. Where total debts do not exceed 150,000 NIS (a fixed figure in the Enforcement Law, not the index-linked insolvency threshold in the SKILL.md Gotchas), that notice is treated as an application to open insolvency proceedings; above it, the office gives information on applying to the insolvency commissioner. Either way the matter moves toward insolvency, which this skill routes out (see the SKILL.md Gotchas).

## Challenge route by file type

| File type | Objection? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bill or check | Yes, form 218 | Grounds include failure of consideration, forgery, theft, no signature, defective endorsement, security check |
| Fixed-sum claim | Yes, form 218 | Adds technical grounds: over the 75,000 NIS ceiling, not calculable by simple arithmetic, or no prior warning of intent served as s.81א1(ב1) requires (registered mail with delivery confirmation, or personal delivery by the creditor's advocate after mail failed) at least 30 days before filing, with a fresh warning after a year |
| Money judgment | No | Paraati, set-aside of a default judgment, appeal, or stay of execution |
| Maintenance | Separate track | Wage protection does not apply. Arrest up to 21 days without an ability examination. No ordinary payment order; past arrears can be spread |
| Performance of an act | Separate track | Out of scope for this skill |
| Mortgage or pledge realisation | Separate track | Out of scope; route to the Kol Zchut mortgage-holder guide or legal aid |

## The five restrictions (Enforcement Law s.66א)

Exit from the country; passport or travel document; special restricted customer (no checks,
no new bank account); charge cards (not an immediate-debit card usable only against a credit balance, or a card loaded only with a protected benefit); founding or holding an interest
in a corporation. Passport and exit restrictions are not imposed where travel is needed for the
health of the debtor or a dependent family member.

There is no driving-licence restriction. A restriction takes effect only 30 days after its
warning is served, or 30 days after imposition if imposed in the debtor's presence (no warning then).
It needs a statutory ground first (s.66ב): evader with debts over 500 NIS after appearing, a maintenance
debt, or presumed evader plus 6 months and over 2,500 NIS, or 1 year and over 500 NIS. Cancellation is on form 214 and is granted where the debt is paid, a payment
order is being complied with, or an approved agreement with the creditor is being complied with.

## Two traps worth restating

1. A payment order, once granted, does **not** lift existing restrictions or attachments.
   Each needs its own request.
2. A restriction can be re-imposed immediately and with no warning if compliance lapses.
