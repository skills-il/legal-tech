# Domain checklist: israeli-hotzaa-lapoal-debtor

Version 3, revised 2026-11-11 (version 2 drafted 2026-09-13). Statute references are to חוק ההוצאה לפועל, התשכ"ז-1967 unless stated.

## Must cover (core)

1. Identify file type (bill, fixed sum, money judgment, maintenance, performance of act) and the challenge route for each. Source: ss.81א, 81א1, 19, 74.
2. Service of the azhara as the anchor for every clock, and defective service as an argument. Source: s.7, s.81א(ג1), s.81א1(ה).
3. Objection within 30 days (bill and fixed-sum files), automatic stay when timely, late-objection package. Source: s.81א(ג),(ג1); s.81א1(ד); Kol Zchut התנגדות לביצוע שטר בהוצאה לפועל.
4. Fixed-sum technical grounds: over 75,000 NIS on filing date, not a fixed sum, no prior 30-day התראה. Source: s.81א1(א),(ב1),(ג).
5. Paraati claim (form 236), no deadline, post-judgment only on judgment files, special-costs risk, stay requires special reasons and usually security. Source: s.19; Kol Zchut טענת "פרעתי".
6. Setting aside a default judgment in the originating court as the route to attack the judgment itself. Source: תקנות סדר הדין האזרחי, התשע"ט-2018, reg. 131 (reg. 130 is the power to give the default judgment); Kol Zchut בקשה לביטול פסק דין שניתן בהיעדר הגנה.
7. Payment-order request within 20 days (or the date set in the azhara), required declaration and confidentiality waiver, period caps (3 or 4 years plus up to 3), right to vary. Source: ss.7א, 7א1, 69ב.
8. Status: חייב בעל יכולת המשתמט and what triggers it (not filing, not appearing). Source: ss.7ג, 66ב(א)(3).
9. Status: חייב מוגבל באמצעים, its spread thresholds (2/3/4 years), interim order, automatic consolidation, restrictions, public register. Source: ss.69ג to 69ח.
10. All five restrictions under s.66א (passport, exit, לקוח מוגבל מיוחד, debit cards, corporation), the health exception, the preconditions in s.66ב, and the 30-day warning before a restriction takes effect. Source: ss.66א, 66ב, 66ג.
11. Cancelling a restriction: automatic on payment, on request with possible security, per file, immediate re-imposition on lapse, leave to appeal. Source: s.66ד; Kol Zchut בקשה לביטול הגבלה שהטיל רשם ההוצאה לפועל.
12. Bank-account and salary attachment: how to request release (form 214 section 4, documents, usual grounds), paying-debtor stay (form 351), third-party money, protected wages for one month after deposit, unattachable National Insurance benefits, debit-only and benefit cards exempt from card restriction. Source: חוק הגנת השכר s.8; חוק הביטוח הלאומי s.303; s.66א1; Kol Zchut עיקול חשבון בנק בהוצאה לפועל.
13. Protected wage table for the current year with the 80% of net rule and the maintenance exception. Source: Kol Zchut שכר עבודה שלא ניתן לעקל או לשעבד.
14. Maintenance track: no s.7א payment order, no limited-means status, arrest up to 21 days without ability examination after a 7-day warning, installment of past maintenance debt conditioned on two months of current payments. Source: ss.74, 69א(ב), 69ב1; Kol Zchut מאסר חייב בתיק מזונות.
15. Deadline calendar covering all clocks: objection 30, payment order 20 (or as set), ability examination 21 (30 in fixed-sum files) / paraati exemption 21, restriction warning 30, 14 days after a rejected payment-order request, 7 days ex parte, 20/15 days appeal, plus the counting rules (day of service not counted; last day on a Friday, Saturday, holiday or recess may move; online filing cut-off 23:59). Source: ss.81א(ג1), 7א, 19, 66ג, 66ה, 67(א1); Interpretation Law s.10; Enforcement (Recess) Regulations reg. 3; Enforcement Regulations regs. 15א2, 27, 128(ב).
16. Routing out to insolvency when the user wants debts discharged, with the current index-linked threshold read from source. Source: חוק חדלות פירעון ושיקום כלכלי, התשע"ח-2018; Kol Zchut חדלות פירעון ושיקום כלכלי לחובות בסכום נמוך.
17. Deemed full service on filing any request, digital viewing, partial service. Source: s.7; Kol Zchut המצאה במסגרת הליכי הוצאה לפועל.
18. Reservist relief temporary order (until 28.2.2027). Source: תקנות ההוצאה לפועל (סדרי דין בלשכות ההוצאה לפועל) (חרבות ברזל) (שירות מילואים) (הוראת שעה), התשפ"ה-2025.

## Should cover (advanced)

0. NEXT CYCLE FIRST (deferred 2026-11-11, unsourced): whether a reg. 27 request to the registrar (7 days) stops or extends the 20-day appeal clock on the original decision. Until sourced the skill must not say either way; a user relying on the 7-day request could miss the appeal window. Source to read: Enforcement Regulations reg. 27, s.80, reg. 119 onward and case law.
1. Register of debtors with means who evade payment (debts over 10,000 NIS, registered-mail invitation, hearing). Source: s.66ה.
2. Short track (מסלול מקוצר) for small obligations, subject to a cap on the debtor's total files. Source: s.20ב (read the current ceilings from the statute before writing any figure).
3. Debtor-initiated consolidation of files, no automatic stay, possible 60-day stay. Source: ss.74ב, 74ג.
4. Arrangement with the creditor staying the file, and the creditor's 7-day duty to report receipts. Source: ss.19א, 19ב.
5. Unattachable movables including household items, livelihood tools and vehicle, disability items, phones and computers. Source: s.22.
6. Over-collection refund and correction of mistaken payments. Source: s.20.
7. Legal aid for debtors and fee exemptions (now in the body, Step 3; income thresholds still unsourced). Source: חוק הסיוע המשפטי, התשל"ב-1972; s.74(ג); Kol Zchut פטור מתשלום אגרות בהוצאה לפועל.
8. Arrest in maintenance files collected by National Insurance, which runs on its own shorter terms. Source: s.70 (read the figures from the statute before writing any).
9. Corporate debtors excluded from the payment-instruction rules. Source: s.69א(א).
10. Limitation arguments via paraati where the execution request was filed after the limitation period. Source: s.19; Kol Zchut טענת "פרעתי".

## Out of scope (explicit)

- Creditor side (opening a file, choosing collection steps). Rationale, re-checked 2026-11-11 (a user may ask; the skill routes out rather than answering): the skill serves a debtor under deadline pressure; creditor strategy is a different user with opposite interests.
- Performance-of-act files, eviction and delivery of property. Rationale, re-checked 2026-11-11 (a user may ask; the skill routes out rather than answering): separate procedure (ss.62 onward) with different stakes and urgency; a housing-loss case needs a lawyer, not a checklist.
- Insolvency proceedings themselves (application, trustee, discharge plan). Rationale, re-checked 2026-11-11 (a user may ask; the skill routes out rather than answering): different forum under חוק חדלות פירעון ושיקום כלכלי; this skill only routes out.
- Traffic, parking and municipal fines collected by מרכז לגביית קנסות, אגרות והוצאות or by administrative collection under פקודת המסים (גבייה). Rationale, re-checked 2026-11-11 (a user may ask; the skill routes out rather than answering): not a registrar file, different challenge routes and bodies.
- Small claims and any civil claim before judgment. Rationale, re-checked 2026-11-11 (a user may ask; the skill routes out rather than answering): there is no enforcement file yet; defence belongs to the court procedure.
- Setting aside a judgment ex debito justitiae (מחובת הצדק) where the claim itself was never validly served, which is not bound by the 30-day reg. 131 window. Rationale, 2026-11-11: the route is case law, not statute or regulation, and no judgment was read this cycle; the body gives the sourced 30-day route and points to legal aid. Re-open once a primary judgment is read.
- Mortgage or pledge realisation files (mimush mashkanta / mashkon). Rationale, 2026-11-11: a distinct file type with its own track (receiver, alternative housing, six-month extension) that would not fit the word cap; the skill names it in Step 1 and routes to the Kol Zchut mortgage-holder guide and legal aid.
