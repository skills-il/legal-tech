# Prior Art Search

Moved out of SKILL.md in v1.6.0. A search by the applicant is a triage step before paying for drafting, not a substitute for a professional search or opinion.

## Databases

| Database | URL | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Israel Patents Online (ILPO) | https://israelpatents.justice.gov.il/en | Israeli applications (once opened at 18 months) and patents |
| WIPO PatentScope | https://patentscope.wipo.int/search/en/search.jsf | PCT applications and national collections |
| Google Patents | https://patents.google.com | Worldwide patents with machine-translated text |
| Espacenet (EPO) | https://worldwide.espacenet.com | European and international collections |

## Search Strategy

1. **IPC classification**: identify the relevant IPC subgroups using WIPO's IPC resource (https://www.wipo.int/en/web/classification-ipc), then filter by them.
2. **Keyword synonyms**: list technical terms, trade names and common alternatives, and run several searches; one query is never enough.
3. **Assignee and inventor search**: search known competitors and related inventors by name in PatentScope and the ILPO system.
4. **Non-patent literature**: for software, also search code repositories, technical blogs and standards; for biotech and pharma, PubMed and journals. Published papers and public code are prior art.
5. **Date cutoff**: prior art is anything made public before the priority date, anywhere in the world, in any language (Patents Law s.4).

## What to record for the attorney

For each relevant hit: the document number, publication date, the passage that comes closest, and in one sentence what your invention does that it does not. This goes into the invention-disclosure memo, and documents you know of must also go into the s.18 list when ILPO asks for it.
