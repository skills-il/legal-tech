# Changelog

## [1.7.0] - 2026-10-25

### Fixed
- Annual leave: most 5-day workplaces are covered by the shortened-work-week extension order, which gives 17 days from year 6 and 23 from year 9; the skill gave only the statute-only column (17 to 20). The 6-day column now shows days of actual absence (14 to 24), not the gross statutory figure.
- Vacation redemption on leaving covers the final year plus the 3 preceding calendar years, not two.
- Convalescence pay is subject to national insurance and health tax (the skill said it was not), and is not part of the pension or severance wage.
- The one-day convalescence deduction for reservist benefits applied to 2024 and 2025 by temporary law; no 2026 law was found, so the skill no longer says it applies in 2026. Public-sector employees get more convalescence days than the private table.
- The severance script under-reported the Section 14 completion: deposits below 8.33% release only the proportional part of severance, so the employer owes the rest even when fund returns push the balance past the statutory figure.
- Section 14 applies automatically to the 6% mandatory deposit; only the completion to 8.33% needs the employer's written notice to the fund. The skill had told users to look for a signed arrangement.
- Overtime day norms: 8.6 hours (7.6 on the short day) on a 5-day week, 8 hours (7 on Friday) on a 6-day week, 40-hour week in the public sector, replacing a flat "8-hour day".
- Notice period: a full month is owed once 12 full months are completed (monthly) or 36 (hourly), and the hourly second and third years start at 12 and 24 months.
- Added the qualifying resignation within 9 months of a birth or adoption, and severance to survivors on an employee's death. Removed an unsourced "up to 150% increased severance" remedy.

### Added
- Holiday work and holiday pay (dmei chagim) as distinct from the weekly-rest premium; global overtime validity conditions; the youth weekly-rest prohibition with the premium still owed.
- Calendar-year seniority, the 200/240-day proration rule, partial-day accrual, and the 18-day youth quota.

### Changed
- Harassment and disability detail moved to `references/harassment-and-disability.md` to keep both skill files under the word cap.

## [1.6.0] - 2026-08-30

### Fixed
- Section 14 was described as a designation the employer simply makes. It is a prohibition with exceptions, operating in practice through the General Authorization (י"פ תשנ"ח 4394), and the 8.33% rate comes from that Authorization rather than from section 14.
- Convalescence pay (dmei havra'a): the private-sector rate for havra'a year 2026 is NIS 451.5 per day, in force, not NIS 418 with an increase "pending an extension order". An employer who paid at 418 owes NIS 33.50 per havra'a day, and the rate follows the havra'a year rather than the payment date.
- Re-sourced the Section 14 claim to the statute.

All notable changes to this skill are documented here.
