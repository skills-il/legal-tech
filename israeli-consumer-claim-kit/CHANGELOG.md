# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-29)

- Telecom: Ministry of Communications complaint route, and no exit fee on cancelling a cellular, internet, landline or TV subscription (Communications Law s.51A, s.51D).
- Goods outside the Warranty Regulations: Sale Law 1968 notice rules (immediate notice, 2 and 4 year limits for hidden defects).
- Warranty objections: importer counts as manufacturer, sticker in place of the certificate, rights pass to a later owner, withdrawal from a long-term service sold with the goods.
- Minors: cancelling a minor's purchase under the Legal Capacity Law (one month, credit purchases).
- Seller insolvency before delivery (Payment Services Law s.18), in-store courses and domestic travel services (reg.2(3)), seven-year limitation, door-to-door deterioration caveat, other proof of purchase in a shop.
- Helper script: jewelry above NIS 3,000 now reported as excluded, Sale Law pointer for in-store defects, telecom and door-to-door notes.
- Description no longer opens with a legal-advice clause (the legal notice section is unchanged).

## 1.0.1 (2026-10-07)

- Technician law: a valid postponement (notice by 20:00 the evening before) sets a new agreed time and does not cancel the compensation; lateness counts from the new time, and only if the postponement does not push the service past its legal deadline.
- No-show: the right depends on the time passing; being home is a matter of proof, not a condition.
- Defence wording aligned with s.18A(f).

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-07)

- Initial release: technician-law compensation, cancellation of distance, door-to-door, in-store and continuing deals (including the 4-month right for seniors, olim and people with disabilities), warranty repair demands, exemplary-damages precondition, complaint routes, and the aggravated-violator mechanism.
