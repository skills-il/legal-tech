# Domain Coverage Checklist (israeli-wills-inheritance)

Generated: 2026-06-18. Revised: 2026-08-01 (v2) via research on: kolzchut.org.il (צוואה בעדים, צוואה, הפקדת צוואה, צו ירושה, צו קיום צוואה), gov.il / inheritance.justice.gov.il (the Inheritance Registrar). Statute: Succession Law 1965 (חוק הירושה תשכ"ה-1965); תקנות הירושה (אגרות הרשם לעניני ירושה), התשנ"ח-1998; חוק מס עזבון (ביטול), התשמ"א-1981.

Scope: help an Israeli (1) draft a legally-valid will (focus on the witnessed will), (2) deposit it with the Inheritance Registrar, and (3) obtain a succession order or a probate order. Drafting binding enduring power of attorney, guardianship, and contested-estate litigation are out of scope.

## Must cover (core)
- [ ] The four will types under the Succession Law: בכתב יד (handwritten), בעדים (witnesses), בפני רשות (before an authority), בעל פה (oral / שכיב מרע). source: kolzchut צוואה. why core: the user must know which form fits.
- [ ] Witnessed will (צוואה בעדים) requirements, the skill's primary deliverable: not legally required to use a lawyer (but a lawyer is recommended); the testator writes/types the wishes, dates it, declares before two witnesses that it is their will, and signs by hand in front of them; the two witnesses confirm in writing on the will. source: kolzchut צוואה בעדים. why core: this is what the skill drafts.
- [ ] Witness disqualification: a witness, and the witness's spouse, cannot be a beneficiary of the will (a bequest to such a person is void). source: kolzchut צוואה בעדים. why core: the single most common way a self-made will is partially invalidated.
- [ ] Will deposit with the Inheritance Registrar (הרשם לענייני ירושה): optional, not required for validity, safeguards the document. source: kolzchut הפקדת צוואה. why core: users ask how to protect the will.
- [ ] Succession order (צו ירושה): filed when the deceased left NO will; heirs receive their share by law (spouse, children, parents per the Succession Law order). source: kolzchut צו ירושה. why core: the no-will path.
- [ ] Probate / will-execution order (צו קיום צוואה): filed when the deceased DID leave a will, to give it binding effect and distribute per its provisions. source: kolzchut צו קיום צוואה. why core: the with-will path.
- [ ] Where and how to apply: online to the Inheritance Registrar (inheritance.justice.gov.il), with the current fees. source: kolzchut צו ירושה, תקנות הירושה (אגרות). why core: the actionable next step.
- [ ] The FULL Registrar fee schedule, not just the common case: all nine rows of the תוספת, the 85% online-filing rule that produces the quoted online figure, the 1 January CPI indexation, the will-deposit fee and its five-year exemption, and the bereaved-family exemptions in regulation 4. source: תקנות הירושה (אגרות הרשם לעניני ירושה). why core: a user depositing a will is usually exempt, and quoting one number that reindexes annually goes stale.
- [ ] Section 25 cure for a defective will: a missing date or signature does not void a will; the Registrar or the court may admit it if the מרכיב היסוד survives. source: חוק הירושה סעיף 25. why core: the skill must never tell a bereaved user a valid will is dead.
- [ ] Section 147: insurance, pension and provident-fund money is outside the estate and passes by the מוטבים designation, which the will does not override. source: חוק הירושה סעיף 147. why core: for most Israelis this is the largest asset, and a will drafted without this warning misleads.
- [ ] Section 55: an unmarried partner (ידוע/ה בציבור) is treated as if left by will what they would have inherited as a spouse. source: חוק הירושה סעיף 55. why core: very large affected population, and the opposite answer is harmful.
- [ ] No estate or inheritance tax in Israel (repealed for deaths after 31 March 1981), with the מס שבח caveat on selling an inherited asset. source: חוק מס עזבון (ביטול), התשמ"א-1981. why core: the single most common user fear on this topic.
- [ ] Section 39: a will confers nothing until a probate order is granted. source: חוק הירושה סעיף 39. why core: sets the expectation that the paper alone moves no bank.

## Should cover (advanced / edge cases)
- [ ] Revoking or changing a will: a later valid will overrides an earlier one; how to update. source: Succession Law. why: common follow-up.
- [ ] Handwritten will (צוואה בכתב יד) requirements: written entirely in the testator's own hand, dated, and signed; no witnesses needed. source: kolzchut. why: the simplest no-witness option.
- [ ] Mutual / reciprocal wills for spouses (צוואה הדדית) and their special revocation rules. source: Succession Law. why: very common for couples.
- [ ] Order of legal heirs when there is no will (spouse share + children / parents / siblings per the parentela system), INCLUDING the Section 11(a)(2) apartment proviso and the Section 11(c) / 104(a)(4) precedence of marital-property settlement over heirs' shares. source: Succession Law. why: needed to explain a צו ירושה outcome, and the proviso often decides who owns the home.
- [ ] Objection / contest (התנגדות לצו) and the other Section 67א transfer grounds (State a party, Attorney General initiates or joins, Public Trustee represents a party, Registrar transfers) that move the file to the Family Court. source: חוק הירושה סעיף 67א. why: sets expectations, and the AG / Public Trustee grounds catch estates with minor heirs.
- [ ] Religious-court forum (Section 155): a rabbinical, sharia or druze court may issue either order where all parties consent in writing. source: חוק הירושה סעיף 155. why: a third door the skill otherwise hides.
- [ ] Disclaimer mechanics (Section 6): written notice to the Registrar, only before distribution, retroactive, only in favour of the deceased's spouse / child / sibling, conditional disclaimer void, minors need court approval, Section 7(c) forfeiture, Section 6א war-related widening. source: חוק הירושה. why: the skill actively recommends disclaiming.
- [ ] Section 8 / Section 27: agreements about a future inheritance and waivers of it are void, and a promise to make or keep a will is unenforceable. source: חוק הירושה. why: a recurring family-agreement question.
- [ ] Section 23(b): the oral will requires a זכרון דברים signed by both witnesses and deposited with the Registrar, and lapses one month after the danger passes. source: חוק הירושה. why: the form is otherwise described without its operative duty.
- [ ] Section 22: a will before an authority may be stated aloud OR handed in writing personally to the authority, is read back and certified on its face, and a notary counts as a judge. source: חוק הירושה. why: the row is otherwise too thin to act on.

## Out of scope (explicit, with rationale)
- Enduring power of attorney (ייפוי כוח מתמשך): requires a certified, specially-trained attorney to draft and file with the Registrar. related skill: israeli-elder-care-navigator covers it.
- Guardianship (אפוטרופסות): a different court process, not part of wills/inheritance drafting.
- Contested-estate litigation between heirs: requires a licensed inheritance lawyer (עורך דין). recommend one.
- Estate / inheritance tax in OTHER countries (US estate tax, etc.): foreign-law, out of scope. NOTE: the ISRAELI position (no estate tax, plus the מס שבח caveat on selling an inherited asset) is now IN scope and is a Must-cover row above; only foreign tax regimes are excluded.
- Complex estates (business interests, trusts, minors' guardianship clauses): recommend a lawyer; the witnessed-will draft is for straightforward bequests.
- Israeli tax computation on the SALE of an inherited asset (מס שבח rates, heir reliefs): name the exposure and route to a tax professional, do not compute it here.

## Deliberately deferred (logged, not yet covered)
- Section 5 יורש פסול (disqualification of an heir who caused the death or destroyed / forged the will).
- The estate-debts chapter (Sections 104, 126, 127, 128) and the creditor-invitation procedure that limits heirs' post-distribution exposure.
- Registering heirs' rights in the Land Registry (Tabu) as a separate application after the order issues.
